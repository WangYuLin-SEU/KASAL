## Original SVG diagrams

The D₄ key-axis diagram is an original illustration based on Wang and Luo, *IEEE Transactions on Image Processing* 33 (2024), Section III, pages 6720–6733. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIP.2024.3515801).

It shows a centered square prism with unequal height and side length, uniform appearance, one four-fold axis and four transverse two-fold axes. The known D₄ type and two key axes determine the remaining axes.

The GDH diagrams are original illustrations based on Zhang et al., *CVPR 2026*, Section 3.1, Equations 1–3, pages 13866–13875. [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR 2026/html/Zhang_KASALv2_Fully_Automatic_3D_Rotational_Symmetry_Classification_and_Axis_Localization_CVPR_2026_paper.html).

They show six geometric degeneration relations: D∞→Dₙ, C∞→Cₙ, D∞→C∞, Dₙ→Cₙ, A₅→A₄ and S₄→A₄, with n≥2. These arrows describe geometric relations rather than an algorithmic processing sequence.
